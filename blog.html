AI in Legal Practice: Tools, Opportunities, and Risks

Overview In this module, you will explore how AI is used in judicial contexts, its potential to improve access to justice, and its risks. You will examine AI tools in courts, assess their outputs, and consider key points for adoption, enabling you to evaluate AI applications in judicial workflows. Learning Outcomes • Recognise how AI is currently used within your context. • Review how different types of AI tools are used in courts across the globe. • Outline the most common risks of AI in the judiciary. • Determine the key decision points for courts when they explore the adoption or procurement of a new AI system. • Analyse an AI tool's judicial task output.

Ensuring AI Fairness and Accuracy in Judicial Contexts

Overview This module examines AI-assisted tools, evidence, and procedures to understand how they can uphold or threaten judicial fairness. By exploring bias, data quality, and system accuracy, you will learn strategies to prevent harm and promote fair outcomes. Learning Outcomes • Define fairness in the use of AI. • Identify the types and sources of bias in AI systems. • Determine the harm caused by algorithmic bias. • Investigate the importance of data as an enabler for safe and fair AI systems. • Recommend key approaches to prevent and mitigate bias at data, model, and institutional levels within a judicial setting. • Evaluate accuracy and error in AI-assisted judicial tools, including their implications for fairness.

Developing AI Transparency Mechanisms in the Justice System

This module focuses on developing transparency mechanisms for AI in the justice system. You will examine black-box AI systems, explore the roles of explainability and interpretability, and consider data governance, privacy, and intellectual property issues. Learning Outcomes • Recognise the importance of transparency in court proceedings. • Identify the key differences and interconnectedness of transparency, explainability, and interpretability. • Determine how data governance practices enable transparency of AI systems. • Demonstrate an understanding of how the use of AI in court proceedings affects the contestability of outcomes.

sep

Building Trust and Accountability When Using AI

Overview In this module, you will examine human oversight, appropriate reliance on AI, and how trust, accuracy, and accountability issues arise across judicial processes. By the end, you will be able to recommend ways to maintain trust and fairness when AI is applied in court. Learning Outcomes • Recognise the different levels of human oversight of AI systems. • Indicate when and to what extent it is appropriate to rely on AI in judicial work. • Investigate how trust, accuracy, and accountability issues arise before, during, and after trial. • Reflect on how the use of AI complicates the enforcement of procedural principles for the judiciary. • Demonstrate an understanding of how the use of AI in court proceedings affects the contestability of the decision-making process and its outcomes.

Promoting Human Rights of Access to Justice and Fair Trial in the Age of AI

This module focuses on protecting human rights in AI-enabled judicial processes. You will examine how AI affects access to justice and fair trials, evaluate limits on automated decisionmaking, and identify gaps in existing frameworks. Learning Outcomes • Indicate how the components of the right to a fair trial apply when AI tools are used in judicial processes. • Determine the limits of delegation to automated systems as an enabler for final, reviewable, and transparent judicial discretion. • Analyse existing access to justice and fair trial frameworks to uncover gaps in protection, monitoring, and remedy.
