The Framework

Building practical AI fluency for legal professionals.

This framework sets out the key competencies that professionals in the legal industry need to safely and productively apply AI in their daily work.

It provides a useful guide for training providers to develop curriculum for their training courses on AI fluency.

Training courses mapped to the framework can be accredited as part of professional development for the legal sector.

Professional Development

From AI awareness to responsible deployment.

A structured progression that supports individual capability, professional practice and organisational AI leadership.

Course Accreditation

To apply for accreditation, check that the course aligns with the learning outcomes and topics of the relevant proficiency level, then submit your course lesson plan using the form available via the QR code.

Proficiency Levels

Three levels of AI fluency.

Each level reflects a different depth of capability and responsibility within the legal profession.

01
Foundation

BASIC

The baseline level of AI fluency that all legal professionals should have to understand and use AI confidently and safely for daily work tasks on a personal level.

For all professionals in the legal industry.
Core Professional Level
02
Professional Practice

INTERMEDIATE

A higher level of fluency in using AI tools, using more sophisticated techniques and higher-order process skills.

For professionals involved in legal operations, knowledge management, or all who are keen to develop their AI fluency.
03
Leadership & Deployment

ADVANCED

Skills needed for AI deployment, including driving AI adoption and management of AI use at team or organisational level.

For professionals with responsibilities to deploy and manage AI at team or organisational level.
