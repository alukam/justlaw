01 — Profile

Law, standards &
technology in motion.

Bridget trained originally in Uganda's common law system before completing her LL.M. in the United States, bringing comparative legal fluency to the challenges created by rapidly evolving technology.

Her work asks a fundamental question: how do law, industry standards, and international norms bend, merge, or break when the technology they were built for stops holding still?

At Ailex Tinkerers, Bridget approaches that question from both a legal and policy perspective, exploring how policy can help bridge the gaps that emerge when technological development moves faster than existing legal frameworks.
