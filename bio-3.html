Abongo Peninah is a Ugandan lawyer and legal and compliance professional with experience in data protection, regulatory compliance, legal research, contract management, and policy development.

She is passionate about the intersection of law and technology and works in data protection and compliance, supporting organisations to strengthen privacy governance and manage regulatory risks.

Her work brings together legal analysis, compliance practice and policy thinking to help organisations understand their obligations and build stronger approaches to privacy and regulatory risk.
